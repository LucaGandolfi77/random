# Chapter 10 — Dark Matter and Dark Energy: Evidence, Equations, and Open Problems

**Level:** Very Advanced  
**Objective:** To present the observational and theoretical status of dark matter and dark energy.

---

Dark matter: rotation curves of spiral galaxies require \( M(r) \propto r \) at large radii, inconsistent with luminous mass profiles. The velocity dispersion in clusters (Zwicky, 1933) and gravitational lensing (Bullet Cluster) confirm non-baryonic matter.

The dark matter density profile (Navarro-Frenk-White) is:

\[
\rho(r) = \frac{\rho_0}{(r/r_s)(1 + r/r_s)^2}
\]

Dark energy: the cosmological constant \( \Lambda \) is the simplest model. Its energy density is \( \rho_\Lambda = \Lambda c^2 / (8\pi G) \approx 7 \times 10^{-27} \mathrm{kg \cdot m^{-3}} \). The fine-tuning problem (why \( \rho_\Lambda \) is so small compared to vacuum energy predictions from quantum field theory, which exceed it by \( \sim 120 \) orders of magnitude) remains unsolved.
