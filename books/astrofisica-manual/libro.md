# Astrophysics: A University Manual

**Title:** Astrophysics — From Stellar Observation to Cosmological Field Equations  
**Author:** Manual for Advanced Study  
**Language:** English  
**Status:** First draft — rigorous, progressive, derivation-based  
**Structure:** 12 Chapters + Mathematical Appendices + References

---
