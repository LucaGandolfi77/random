# Chapter 12 — The Last Gate

**POV:** Ciro · **Place:** Milan (Bollate, the gate, the warehouse of the Germoglio) · **Story time:** 25 September 2025 – October 2025
**Words:** ~1.250 / translation of `il-permesso/capitoli/12.md` ✅

---

## Draft

> "…and shipwreck is sweet to me in this sea."
>
> — Giacomo Leopardi, *L'infinito*

On the morning of the twenty-fifth of September the gate opened at half past seven, and the gate, at half past seven, opened to make you go out: it was the first time in six years the gate opened to make you go out. I didn't know it when I went out, the first of March two years before: I believed it opened to let you go, and the gate opened to make you come back in. That day it opened to make you go out: and going out, at thirty-six, is the going out you don't expect: you receive it.

The count was at six, and the count, that evening, wasn't there: the count, for the first time in six years, didn't count me. The sheet was folded in four, and the sheet said my name pronounced wrong, and the writing: *work release*. The sheet expired on the twenty-fifth of September, and the sheet, at thirty-six, doesn't renew: it's kept. I still keep it. It's in a pocket of a notebook, and I keep it the way you keep proofs: you don't use them, you show them.

Liberation is the thing you don't buy: you receive it. And the receipt, at thirty-six, was the receipt of six years of sentence: the receipt you keep. The gate closed at half past five, and the gate, for the first time in six years, closed without me inside: the gate closed, and the closing, at thirty-six, is the closing you don't feel: you feel it later, and later, that evening, was the after of freedom.

Freedom was the infinite. In the books Adele had given me, the hedge wasn't a cage: it was the measure that made the infinite possible (Leopardi, *L'infinito*), and the infinite, without a hedge, doesn't free: it frightens. I had the infinite, and I didn't have the hedge: and the vertigo, at thirty-six, is the vertigo I am learning to look at. Shipwreck, there, was sweet (Leopardi, *L'infinito*), and the sweet, at thirty-six, isn't said: it's kept.

Man, the books say, gets used to everything (Dostoevsky): the cold, the count, the gate. Even freedom. And the habit of freedom, at thirty-six, is the thing I am learning: you learn it ten hours a day, with the garden, with the books, with the subjunctive. Freedom isn't got used to: it's built. And the building, at thirty-six, is the building of the straight path: the straight path isn't found again. It's remade.

Gennaro called again on a Friday in October, from the barra, and calling again, at thirty-six, is the calling that's kept: it's kept and it isn't contested. — The place — Gennaro said, with the code's voice. — The last time. —

— I can't — I said. — I said I can't. Twice. —

— And the two times — Gennaro said, and the laugh was the code's laugh: softly. — The two times, at thirty-six, aren't a no. They're an account. —

— They're a no — I said. — And the no is kept. —

It was the truth, and the truth, said three times, isn't contested: it's kept. Gennaro didn't call again, and the not calling again, at thirty-six, is the thing that's kept: it's kept the way doors that close are kept. The no, in the evening, is kept: it's kept the way the things you don't buy are kept.

The contract the cooperative signed the week after, and the contract said *permanent*: the contract, at thirty-six, was the first contract of my life. In the books of the girl I read, the permanent contract is the thing you buy: you buy it with the exam. Mine was the contract you receive: you receive it with the hands, and hands, at thirty-six, are the hands of someone who does everything: they're kept. The cooperative kept it, and keeping, at thirty-six, is the keeping that isn't bought: it's received.

The first evening of freedom I did at the garden, with the sack and the gloves, and the first evening, at thirty-six, is the evening you don't wait for: you do it. The gate closed at half past five, and the gate, for the first time in six years, closed without me inside.

And I was outside, and the outside, at thirty-six, is the outside you have: you have it, or you don't, and I had it.
