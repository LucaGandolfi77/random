# Quantum Mechanics: A University Manual

**Title:** Quantum Mechanics — From Postulates to Fields  
**Language:** English  
**Status:** First draft — rigorous, progressive, derivation-based, with integrated literary reflections  
**Structure:** 12 Chapters + Mathematical Appendices + References
