## Draft status

- Expanded draft in progress; current manuscript: approximately 42,200 words across 83 chapter files. Target: 70,000–80,000 words, approximately 28–32 full-length chapters (many current files are interludes and will be consolidated during structural editing).
- The draft has grown through new subplots for Imani, Lio, Mina, Reva, and Leona, alongside deeper family and relationship arcs. It remains below traditional novel length; continue deepening scenes and adding chapters without padding through repetition. A structural pass is still required.
- References to classic works are thematic. Verify any quoted translations against chosen editions before publication.

## Remaining long-form expansion

To reach the target without stretching scenes artificially, build the remaining ~27,800 words through substantial scene sequences:

1. **Mara before Bellwether’s service (4–5k):** clinical training, her father’s death, the sisters’ different ways of grieving, and the origin of Mara’s faith in competence.
2. **Nell and Reva (4–5k):** more of Ilyana’s life, the practical and emotional strain of guardianship, and Nell’s map as a contested community project.
3. **Lio at the harbor (4–5k):** the union, the employer’s safety case, a concrete workplace incident, and the difficult limits of accommodations.
4. **Jonah and Tomas (4–5k):** childhood, the archive search, Mina’s memories, and Jonah’s evolving ethics around private records.
5. **The Service under pressure (5–6k):** clinic cases, staff shortages, Imani’s professional formation, Sera’s review, and the consequences of competing measures of success.
6. **Mara and Jonah (4–5k):** their separation and reunion through scenes, not summary; intimacy, conflict, and the ordinary negotiation of trust.
7. **The city’s political struggle (5–6k):** the budget, Leona and Rusk, the press, privacy disputes, and opposition to reform.
8. **Hearing and aftermath (5–6k):** more witnesses, deliberation, consequences for Sera, and a prolonged pilot with setbacks and revision.
9. **Resolution (3–4k):** allow Nell, Lio, Imani, and Mara each a distinct next step; preserve ambiguity rather than equating a hopeful ending with cure.
