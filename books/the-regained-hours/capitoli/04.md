## Draft

January came, and January, at Meridian, is the month of the target: the firm asks for more, and the asking is the asking of the firm. Dana called it the annual objective, and the annual objective, at thirty-eight, was the objective of the firm: 2,200 hours. Two thousand two hundred, and the two hundred — the difference — were the hours the machine had given me, given back. The machine gave six hundred a year; the firm asked for six hundred more; and the asking, at thirty-eight, was the asking of the firm: the machine gives, and the firm takes, and the taking has a name, and the name is the objective.

The hours were eaten again. The reports went through the assistant in eleven minutes, and the eleven minutes were the thing the firm loved: the firm called it efficiency, and the efficiency, at thirty-eight, was the efficiency of the hours eaten twice. I gave the machine the claims; the machine gave me the hours; the firm gave me the claims again — and the giving was the thing the floor should have feared, and the floor feared the machine. The floor feared the wrong thing. The machine doesn't eat hours: the machine gives them. The firm eats them — and the eating, at thirty-eight, had a name, and the name was the objective.

The reports arrived on the screen the way the madeleine arrives: by taste, and without asking. The past doesn't call: it arrives, by smell (Proust), and the smell of the reports was the smell of the office — the paper, the ink, the coffee that nobody chooses. The madeleine, in the books, was a cake, and the cake brought back a life. The assistant was a machine, and the machine brought back hours. The madeleine's time was the past; the machine's time was the future: the hours that came back were the hours I hadn't had yet — and the hadn't, at thirty-eight, was the thing I filled with the firm's claims. The madeleine was a machine, and I didn't know it yet: the knowing, I learned later, was the whole of the year.

And the madeleine, in the office, was the kitchen: my mother's kitchen, the kitchen of the bread — the bread she baked on Saturdays, and the smell of the bread was in the office, and the office was in the smell, and the smell was in the reports, and the reports were in the machine, and the machine was in the eleven minutes — and the eleven minutes, in January, were the eleven minutes of the kitchen: the kitchen of Margaret, the mother who baked, the mother who was gone ten years, and the gone was in the smell, and the smell was in the flour, and the flour was in the bread, and the bread was in the kitchen, and the kitchen was the thing I didn't have anymore: I had the counter. The counter, in the kitchen of Margaret, was the thing that didn't exist — and the not existing, at thirty-eight, was the thing the counter ate: the counter ate the kitchen, and the kitchen was the thing I didn't bake in anymore, and the baking was the thing I didn't do, and the doing was the thing the machine came to give back. The machine gave the hours; the kitchen was the thing the hours were for; and the for, at thirty-eight, was the thing I didn't know.

The costs of London didn't go down. The flat in Islington took sixteen hundred, the school run took two hundred, the tube took a hundred and forty, the food took three hundred, the bills took a hundred — and the counter, in the top right corner, went up. The going up didn't go down either: the going up was the going up of the firm, and the firm's going up, at thirty-eight, was the thing I had: the hours, and the hours' cost, and the cost's hours. The counter said 2,200, and the 2,200 were the thing I kept: the hours. The things you keep, you don't count — the books of the collection say it, and the books were right, and I was counting. The counting was the thing I kept, and the keeping of the counting, at thirty-eight, was the thing that cost me the most: not the machine. The counting.

The evening came, and the smell of the office was the smell of the firm: the paper, the ink, the coffee that nobody chooses. The past doesn't call: it arrives, by smell (Proust). And the smell, that evening, was the firm's.
