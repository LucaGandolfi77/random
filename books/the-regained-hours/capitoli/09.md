## Draft

The train arrived at two, and the two, on Sunday, was the two of the train that didn't wait: the stroke didn't wait. The hospital was in York, and the hospital, at two, was the hospital of the light: the light of the ward, the light of the corridor, the light that doesn't count. The ward was the fourth, and the ward, at seventy-two, was the ward of the things you keep: the light, the bed, the window. The window gave on the garden — no: the window gave on the car park, and the car park, at seventy-two, was the car park that doesn't count. The garden was the thing Edward kept, and the garden was not in the window: the garden was at home, and the home was the thing that kept the garden.

Edward was in the bed, and Edward's face was the face of the man who keeps: the keeping of Edward, at seventy-two, was the thing the stroke had touched. The right hand didn't keep: the right hand was the hand of the garden, and the hand, at seventy-two, was the hand that didn't move — the hand moved, and the moving was the thing the doctors counted: the doctors counted the hand, and the counting of the hand, at thirty-eight, was the counting of the daughter who counts. The hand didn't count. The hand kept.

The days, in the ward, were the days I crossed. The days are not lived: they are crossed — the books say it (Proust), and the books say it about the days of May. The days of the ward were the days of June, and the days of June, at thirty-eight, were the days of the daughter who counts: the days were crossed, and the crossing was the thing I did instead of the living. The living was the thing Edward did: Edward lived the ward, and the living, at seventy-two, was the living of the man who keeps. The keeping of the ward, at seventy-two, was the thing the counter didn't know: the counter counted, and the ward kept.

The nurse was the nurse who asks, and the nurse, at thirty-eight, was the nurse who asks the question that is not the counter: — How is he today? — And the question, at thirty-eight, was the question I had instead of the counter: the question was the thing I had, and the having of the question, at thirty-eight, was the thing I learned. The question doesn't count. The question keeps. And the keeping of the question, at thirty-eight, was the thing the machine couldn't do: the machine gives hours; the question keeps them.

The neighbour was at the ward, on the Wednesday, with the tomatoes from the garden: the tomatoes were the garden's, and the tomatoes, at seventy-two, were the thing the neighbour kept. The neighbour kept the garden, and the keeping of the neighbour, at seventy-two, was the thing Edward owed: the garden was the thing you keep, and the keeping was the thing you give. The neighbour gave the tomatoes, and the giving, at thirty-eight, was the thing I learned in the ward: the giving doesn't count. The giving keeps. And the keeping of the giving, at thirty-eight, was the thing the counter couldn't say: the counter counted, and the giving kept.

The evening came, and the ward's light was the light of the ward: the light that doesn't count. The days, in the ward, were the days I should have had in the garden. And the days I should have had, at thirty-eight, were the hours the machine had given me — and I had given them away.
