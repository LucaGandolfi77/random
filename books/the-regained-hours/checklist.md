# ✅ Checklist — Revision and publication — *The Regained Hours*

> For the end of the draft. Proceed in order: each phase refines the previous one.

## 1. Rest phase

- [ ] Let the draft rest for 2–4 weeks before rereading

## 2. Structural revision (rewriting)

- [ ] Full reading, noting plot, pace, coherence problems
- [ ] Verify all main beats (see bible): the assistant → the false triumph → the kept hours
- [ ] Verify Iris's arc (from who gives the hours away to who keeps them) and the arc of the voice
- [ ] Cut scenes that don't serve the plot or the characters
- [ ] Verify coherence: names, places, timeline (timeline in the bible), numbers (1,600 hours, 600 hours, £1,600)
- [ ] Verify that every subplot opens **and** closes (table 4.3 of the bible)
- [ ] Verify the exaltation lands: surface critique, exalting core

## 3. Line editing and proofreading

- [ ] Sentence-by-sentence revision: rhythm, repetitions, filler words
- [ ] Spelling and punctuation check (also reading aloud)
- [ ] Tense and narrative person check (first person, uniform everywhere)
- [ ] Dialogue check: punctuation, natural lines
- [ ] Verify the allusions in parentheses are present but never explained
- [ ] Beta reader feedback implemented

## 4. Formatting

- [ ] Uniform chapter titles
- [ ] Uniform quotation marks and dashes
- [ ] Front matter and back matter complete
- [ ] EPUB/PDF conversion (if self-publishing)
- [ ] Cover

## 5. Publication

- [ ] ISBN / publication code
- [ ] Back cover + final blurb
- [ ] Metadata (genre, keywords, categories)
- [ ] Upload to the platform / submission to the publisher
- [ ] Price chosen

## 6. Promotion

- [ ] Author page
- [ ] Announcement on social channels / newsletter
- [ ] Review copies sent to readers and critics
- [ ] Launch event (optional)

---

**Golden rule:** done > perfect. First a complete draft, then perfection.
