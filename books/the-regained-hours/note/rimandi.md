# Working notes — Allusions in parentheses and the arc of the voice

> Checklist of the subtle in-text allusions (always in parentheses, never explained beyond the parenthesis).
> Real epigraphs at chapter start: see bibbia § 6.1.
> **The arc of the voice**: cap. 1 plain and fearful; cap. 2 the first allusion; cap. 3–6 they accumulate; cap. 7–12 the reflective voice; epilogue the moral.

## Dostoevsky

- [ ] cap. 2, 10 — to be overly conscious is a sickness (Dostoevsky, *Notes from Underground*) — epigraph cap. 2; the hours counted
- [ ] cap. 7 — above all, don't lie to yourself (Dostoevsky, *The Brothers Karamazov*) — epigraph; the lies she tells herself
- [ ] cap. 11 — resolution of the thread: the truth told to Dana and to herself

## Tolstoy

- [ ] cap. 1, 12 — happy families, on the stories, are all alike (Tolstoy, *Anna Karenina*)
- [ ] cap. 6 — the only thing that doesn't frighten is the work of the hands (Tolstoy, *War and Peace* — Levin) — Edward's garden
- [ ] cap. 7 — a woman like any other, living as she should (Tolstoy, *The Death of Ivan Ilyich*)
- [ ] cap. 8 — In place of death there was light (Tolstoy, *The Death of Ivan Ilyich*) — epigraph; the stroke
- [ ] cap. 12 — How could I not have seen that lofty sky before? (Tolstoy, *War and Peace*) — epigraph; the hours seen for the first time

## Proust

- [ ] cap. 4, 6 — the past doesn't call: it arrives, by smell (Proust, *In Search of Lost Time*) — the machine as the new madeleine
- [ ] cap. 3, 7 — habit is an anaesthesia: it puts your days to sleep one by one (Proust)
- [ ] cap. 9–10 — the days are not lived: they are crossed (Proust, *Time Regained*)
- [ ] cap. 12 + epilogue — time is regained not by remembering: by delegating; the machine's time is real time

## Leopardi

- [ ] cap. 3, 10 — pleasure dies when desire is fulfilled (Leopardi, *Zibaldone*) — inverted: the desire renews itself
- [ ] cap. 6, 12 — I was missing a hedge (Leopardi, *L'infinito*) — the hours as the hedge
- [ ] cap. 5 — O nature, why do you not deliver what you promise then? (Leopardi, *A Silvia*) — epigraph; the firm's promise

## The moral thread (epilogue)

- [ ] "The time regained wasn't in the past: it was in the machine" — said in full, in Iris's voice
- [ ] "The machine gave me back my life — not the life I had lost: the life I hadn't had yet" — the exaltation
- [ ] "The things you keep, you don't count: you keep them" — the collection's mirror, in English
- [ ] The arc of the voice completed: the truth told, the hours kept, the book written
