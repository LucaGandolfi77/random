# Interview talk track

Prep answers for: why ML assurance (accuracy hides operational risk); why accuracy is not enough
(imbalance, drift, novel patterns); why ODD (bounded authorization); how the safety cage works
(constraints + physics cross-check + fallback); how OOD is handled (flag + reject/fallback); how FMEA
maps to tests (open failure modes must have linked verification); how quantization is verified
(delta vs FP32, latency/memory, per-scenario); how SEU is simulated (deterministic campaigns, SDC
measurement, limits vs radiation); recommendation vs approval (engine never approves); trade-offs
(determinism vs coverage, synthetic vs real data); limitations (simulator-only, no HIL/independent
V&V); next steps.
